# Customer-Service Training Package Pattern

Use this reference when a meeting or operations task requires employee onboarding materials, facilitator guidance, and step-by-step walkthroughs.

## Required package

- Training schedule with module order, duration, exit gate, and completion evidence.
- Facilitator guide: what to demonstrate, observe, score, and escalate.
- Trainee walkthroughs: detailed system and process steps.
- Manager readiness/sign-off checklist.
- Facilitator PowerPoint and trainee walkthrough PowerPoint for live instruction.
- Scenario exercises tied to policy and escalation rules.

## Policy integration

Treat the governing Terms and Conditions or policy page as an operating source of truth. For each module, teach:

1. The applicable rule.
2. The decision point.
3. Required documentation.
4. Escalation trigger.
5. Evidence of completion.

For travel customer service training, explicitly cover payment/refund rules, cancellation timing, Trip Protection handling, failed payments, chargebacks, supplier/airline boundaries, package requirements, pricing exceptions, and manager approval before publication.

## Walkthrough standard

For each system or process, document:

1. Open the correct system/record.
2. Identify request type and business outcome.
3. Verify policy/source-of-truth material.
4. Perform only authorized actions.
5. Record owner, status, policy basis, next action, and target date.
6. Escalate ambiguity, financial decisions, exceptions, or publication actions.
7. Confirm completion.

Mark company-specific UI labels, permissions, and routing as items to confirm; do not invent exact buttons when the source material does not show them.

## Validation

Validate every DOCX and PPTX, read back deck slide counts/outlines, and package office files in a ZIP when Windows mis-associates direct attachments with Media Player. Provide a browser download page when necessary.
