# Training Artifact Pre-Delivery Checklist

- Audience and job behaviors are explicit.
- Facilitator and trainee materials are distinct.
- Plain-language labels are used; replace abstract corporate jargon.
- Each slide has a clear purpose and a bounded amount of text.
- Policy formulas, approval gates, escalation triggers, and documentation fields are explicit.
- Sensitive-data and AI-use boundaries are included.
- Personal/customer data is excluded from examples unless approved.
- `pptx_read.py --outline` confirms slide count, text, notes, and role distinction.
- DOCX/PDF validation passes.
- Visual render/projector check is completed when rendering tools are available; otherwise disclose the limitation.
- Independent QA review is complete and all blockers are resolved.
- Final filenames are clear and draft/QA-fixed variants are not confused.
